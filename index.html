About Me
MRR

I'm a physics graduate from University of Jember with a strong foundation in quantitative analysis, experimental design, and scientific communication — skills I'm actively channeling into data analytics.

My undergraduate research on composite material films led to a peer-reviewed publication in RSC Advances (Q1). Working with characterization data — FTIR spectra, stress-strain curves, XRD patterns — gave me an early appreciation for what careful data interpretation can reveal. That curiosity is now pointed at business and social datasets.

Two years as a teaching assistant across eight technical courses sharpened how I explain complex things clearly. I think that's as useful in a data role as any technical skill.
